Buuzz App

I designed the ride from sign-up to parking, with cost and range visible before a rider unlocks a bike.

Role
Product Designer
Platform
iOS / Android
Year
2022

The challenge

Riders needed to understand registration, cost, battery range, and parking rules before committing to a ride.

Outcome

The design makes onboarding, pricing, bike range, and parking guidance visible throughout the ride.

In this studyApproachKey decisionSolution

The Approach

Reduce uncertainty before a ride

A rider needs more than a nearby bike: they need to know its range, what the ride will cost, and where they can park before committing.

Break onboarding into steps

Phone verification, identity checks, and wallet setup are presented separately, with progress visible as the rider moves through them.

Design for the whole journey

I connected discovery, the active ride, parking guidance, and the final summary so important information appears when it is needed.

The Key Decision

Registration and payment can feel overwhelming when presented as one long form.

I separated phone verification, identity checks, and wallet setup into clear steps.

Riders see what comes next before moving on.

The Solution

Find a bike

See nearby bikes, battery level, and estimated range before choosing.

Ride with confidence

A live ride screen shows time, distance, and running cost.

Park and finish

If a rider tries to end outside a designated zone, the app explains the parking rule before they finish. A summary then shows the ride time, distance, and fare.
