Post Planning Calendar & Post Ideas

I introduced Post Ideas so teams could put an idea on the calendar before writing a draft.

Role
Senior UI/UX Designer
Platform
Web, enterprise intranet
Year
2024

The challenge

Content teams planned posts in spreadsheets while drafts and scheduled posts were scattered across the product.

Outcome

A shared planning view brings ideas, drafts, and scheduled posts together, with conflict warnings and bulk import.

In this studyApproachKey decisionSolution

The Approach

Connect the scattered work

Drafts and scheduled posts were managed in separate areas, while early ideas often stayed in spreadsheets. I mapped these states into one planning flow.

Plan before writing

A calendar alone could not show work that had not yet become a draft. I defined a lightweight idea state to bridge that gap.

Build on existing publishing

The planning views sit on top of the existing editor and publishing flow, so teams can plan without relearning how to publish.

The Key Decision

Planning starts before a draft exists.

I introduced Post Ideas: lightweight entries that can be dated, scheduled, and converted into posts.

Ideas without dates stay in an Unplanned panel until the team is ready to schedule them.

The Solution

Plan together

A shared calendar brings ideas, drafts, and scheduled posts together. Colour-coded states help teams understand what is ready and what still needs work.

Schedule visually

Teams can drag unscheduled items onto the calendar, adjust dates, and spot warnings when planned posts overlap.

Move from idea to post

Create a post from an idea with its title and audience already filled in.
